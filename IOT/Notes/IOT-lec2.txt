IOT : Internet Of Thing

- Components of IOT :
1. sensor : detects environment condition
2. controller: process sensor info and make decision 
3. network: transfer reading and commands
4. cloud/app: displays or store readings and command
5. actuator: perform a physical actions

- Characteristics of IOT:
 
1. Connectivity
2. sensing
3. intelligence
4. Automation
5. scalability
6. Heterogeneity

- Scopes & Applications of IOT: 
1. Area
2. Healthcare
3. Agriculture
4. Transportation
5. Smart Cities
6. Industry
7. Smart Homes
